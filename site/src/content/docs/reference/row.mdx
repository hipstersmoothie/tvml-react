## Overview

A `divTemplate` places children in regions, and the children of a region stack vertically. A
`<row>` as wide as the screen centers its cells, which makes it the one reliable building
block: `Row` renders one, adds up its children's widths, and fills the rest of the safe area
with a spacer to align them.

<Example
  name="row-alignment"
  capture="/screens/layout.jpg"
  caption="The kitchen-sink Layout screen, which includes this example"
/>

`Row` can measure `Cell`, `Spacer`, `Image`, `Stack` and `Box` children. With anything else,
it centers its content instead (and warns in development). A row wider than the safe area
warns too; pass `bleed` to use the whole screen.

<Note>
Vertical margins on a row's children are ignored. Space rows with `marginTop` and
`marginBottom`.
</Note>
