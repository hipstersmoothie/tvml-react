## Overview

```tsx
<PlayerOverlay>
  <divTemplate>
    <Backdrop src={episode.artwork} dim={0.4} />
  </divTemplate>
</PlayerOverlay>
```

The overlay's child is a template, usually a free-form `divTemplate`, rendered into its own
document from your app's tree, so it can use your state and hooks. Mount it before or during
playback.

A plain overlay is drawn over the video and doesn't take input. With `interactive`, it takes focus
when the user swipes down, for controls of your own.

Without a `PlayerProvider`, pass the player from `usePlayer` as `player`.
