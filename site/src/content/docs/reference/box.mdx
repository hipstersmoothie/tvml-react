## Overview

Children of a box take a `position`: `top-left`, `top`, `top-right`, `left`, `center`,
`right`, `bottom-left`, `bottom` or `bottom-right`. They can overlap, so a caption and a badge
can sit over an image that fills the box.

<Note>
`Box` renders an `<organizer>`. A position sets both `tv-position` and the matching
`tv-align`; with `tv-position` alone, children at the top, center and bottom sit at the left
of their region.
</Note>

## Properties

<Prop name="width" type="number">Width in points.</Prop>
<Prop name="height" type="number">Height in points.</Prop>
<Prop name="offset" type="number">Extra space before the box in a row.</Prop>
<Prop name="position" type="Position">Where the box goes inside another `Box`.</Prop>
<Prop name="children" type="ReactNode">`Cell`s, `Image`s and `Stack`s, each with a `position` (default center).</Prop>
