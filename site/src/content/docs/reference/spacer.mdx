## Overview

A blank cell of a fixed width, for pushing a group of cells to the far side of a row:

```tsx
<Row>
  <Cell width={600}>Title</Cell>
  <Spacer width={CONTENT_WIDTH - 600 - 300} />
  <Cell width={300} align="right">12:41</Cell>
</Row>
```

For a single group, `Row`'s `align` does this for you.

## Properties

<Prop name="width" type="number">Width in points.</Prop>
