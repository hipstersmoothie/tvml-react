## Overview

A cell is a `<text>` with a width. Without a `width`, it's sized to its text with
`measureText`: exact when the app was launched with `TVMLReact.launch`, estimated otherwise.
`cellWidth(props)` returns the same number, for laying out the rest of a row around it.

<Example name="row-alignment" />

Inside a [`Stack`](/reference/stack/), cells take the stack's width and alignment. Inside a
[`Box`](/reference/box/), give them a `position`.

## Properties

<Prop name="children" type="string | number">The text. `\n` starts a new line.</Prop>
<Prop name="width" type="number">Width in points. Defaults to the text's width.</Prop>
<Prop name="align" type={`"left" | "center" | "right"`} default={`"left"`}>Text alignment within the cell.</Prop>
<Prop name="size" type="number">Font size in points.</Prop>
<Prop name="weight" type={`"ultralight" | "thin" | "light" | "regular" | "medium" | "semibold" | "bold" | "heavy"`}>Font weight.</Prop>
<Prop name="color" type="string">Text color. Defaults to the `Screen`'s.</Prop>
<Prop name="background" type="string">A fill behind the text. Corners can't be rounded.</Prop>
<Prop name="height" type="number">A fixed height; the text is vertically centered.</Prop>
<Prop name="lines" type="number" default="1">Lines before truncating with an ellipsis.</Prop>
<Prop name="offset" type="number">Extra space before the cell, on top of the row's gap.</Prop>
<Prop name="position" type="Position">Where the cell goes inside a `Box`.</Prop>
